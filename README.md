# 📊 Customer Churn Analytics Dashboard (Power BI)

## 🧭 Project Overview

This project analyzes customer churn behavior for a telecom company using **Power BI**.
The goal is to identify key churn drivers, segment high-risk customers, and quantify potential revenue loss to support data-driven retention strategies.

The dashboard provides an executive-level view of churn performance along with detailed insights into customer tenure, contract type, and service usage patterns.


## 🎯 Business Objectives

* Measure overall churn performance
* Identify customer segments with high churn risk
* Quantify revenue at risk due to churn
* Provide actionable insights to improve retention


## 📈 Key KPIs

* **Total Customers:** Overall customer base size
* **Churned Customers:** Number of customers who left
* **Churn Rate (%):** Percentage of customers churned
* **Revenue at Risk:** Potential revenue loss from churned customers
* **ARPU (Average Revenue Per User):** Revenue efficiency metric


## 🔍 Dashboard Insights

* Month-to-month contract customers show the highest churn
* Majority of churn occurs within the first year of tenure
* Fiber optic internet users exhibit higher churn probability
* High-value customers contribute significantly to revenue risk


## 🧰 Tools & Technologies
